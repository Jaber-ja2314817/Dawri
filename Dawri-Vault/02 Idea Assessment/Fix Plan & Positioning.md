## Positioning options

**A — Patients (recommended for the pitch)**
> For walk-in patients at Qatar's health centres who lose time queuing at the front desk before they even reach a nurse, **Dawri** lets them check in from the car in Arabic or English and walk straight to triage.
> Evidence: walk-ins ≈ 69% of PHCC visits; no digital walk-in path in Nar'aakom or Lbaih.

**B — The health system (for the "how does this go live" slide)**
> For PHCC and HMC, who already have patient apps but still run a manual front desk, **Dawri** is an AI front-desk module that plugs into Nar'aakom/Lbaih and the existing queue system.
> Evidence: both apps cover appointments/records only; 300k+ Lbaih downloads show patients are already on mobile.

**C — Sovereign AI angle (for the "why HF / why Qatar" slide)**
> Built on **Fanar**, Qatar's own open Arabic model from QCRI, so patient conversations can stay on infrastructure inside Qatar.

**Recommendation:** lead with **A**, close with **B + C**.

## Tagline options
- *Arrive, don't register.* (EN) / *تعال، دورك جاهز.* (AR — "Come, your turn is ready.")
- *Skip the desk, not the line.* — signals fairness: you still wait your turn.

## Naming note
Don't use "Hamad", "PHCC", "Nar'aakom" or "Lbaih" in the product name or logo. Saying "designed to plug into Nar'aakom" in the pitch is fine.
