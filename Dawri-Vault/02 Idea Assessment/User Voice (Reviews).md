## Themes

### 1. Logging in is painful — **strongest theme** (4 sources)
- Google Play: users report the app saying the service is "temporarily unavailable" even with correct credentials, after reinstalling.
- App Store (Arabic review): login through the Tawtheeq account keeps failing and locks the account — the reviewer says Nar'aakom has the same problem.
- App Store (English review): account creation kicked back to the start five times.
- Review aggregator (mwm.ai): summary notes recent updates made authentication more complex.

**Lesson for Dawri:** our login must be one step. For the demo: QID + OTP 0000. In the pitch: "check-in should work even when the patient is stressed and sick — one tap after first setup (biometric)."

### 2. Navigation and design feel heavy — thin (1 source)
- An aggregator-quoted review asks for a complete redesign and a proper lab-results dashboard; another says the intro is unnecessarily long.

**Lesson:** our patient flow should be one conversation, not menus. Thin theme — don't present it as a trend.

### 3. Getting seen without an appointment is hard — medium (Reddit + PHCC data + press)
- Reddit (2026 snapshot): residents say health centres discourage walk-ins unless urgent; one recalls you used to walk in with minimal waiting.
- PHCC 2025 snapshot: walk-ins are the majority of visits; appointment wait ~14 days. See [[Competitor Landscape]].

**Lesson:** this is our core problem. Walk-ins happen anyway; make them orderly instead of discouraged.

### 4. "Love it, but…" — positives to keep
- Several Play reviews praise having appointments, records and lab results in one place.

**Lesson:** don't rebuild records/appointments. Integrate with Lbaih/Nar'aakom, own the front desk.

## What users asked for, in their words (paraphrased where long)
- Easier login (multiple)
- Lab-results dashboard that highlights abnormal values (1)
- Shorter onboarding (1)

## Unsolved (positioning material)
- Walk-in patients have **no digital path at all** — not in either app.
- Elderly / non-smartphone patients still depend on the desk → Dawri kiosk mode + family (dependent) check-in.
