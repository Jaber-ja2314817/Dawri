# Pitch (6 slides)

| # | Slide | Say (short) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Problem** — photo-free: a big number "≈69% walk-in" + "front desk → triage → doctor → pharmacy = 4 queues" | Walk-ins are most of PHCC's visits. Every one starts at a desk that re-types what the patient will repeat to the nurse. |
| 2 | **What exists** — Nar'aakom & Lbaih: appointments, records, cards ✅ · walk-in check-in ❌ · live queue ❌ · intake ❌ | Patients are already on mobile (Lbaih passed 300k downloads). The front desk is the part nobody digitised. |
| 3 | **Dawri** — the journey diagram from [[Patient Journey]] | Check in from the car, in Arabic or English. Your place activates when you arrive. Walk straight to triage. |
| 4 | **Live demo** | [[Demo Script]] |
| 5 | **Why AI, and how it stays safe** — layered guardrails diagram from [[Safety Guardrails]] | AI listens and summarises; rules catch emergencies first; nurses confirm; the queue is never touched by the model. |
| 6 | **Why Qatar, what's next** — roadmap: pilot one PHCC centre → Nar'aakom module → same core for other government service centres ([[Vision - Dawri Platform]]) | Built on Qatar's own open model so data can stay in Qatar. Next: a pilot at one centre. Then: today it's your turn at the health centre — tomorrow, anywhere in Qatar you take a number. |

## Impact metrics to promise for a pilot (not claim as results)
- Minutes from arrival to triage (front-desk time removed)
- % of visits deflected (refills) or redirected (emergencies)
- Released no-show places per day
- Patient satisfaction with waiting (PHCC already measures this)
